Common questions

Three things patients ask before coming in.

Appointments

Do I have to book?

No. Walk-ins are welcome every day we are open. Booking simply means less waiting once you arrive.

Children

Can I bring my child?

Yes. We have a child-friendly room and picture-based charts, so children who cannot yet read can still be tested properly.

Urgent

Can I come today?

For sudden vision loss, pain, trauma or severe redness — call us straight away. Same-day review is prioritised.
