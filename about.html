Clinic story

Designed around services, screening and continuity of care.

We built Ibotombi Eye Care to make consultation, diagnosis and treatment planning feel straightforward — and, above all, humane. You will find out quickly what care is available, whether it applies to you, and exactly how to come and see us.

Every patient pathway — children's myopia, adult glaucoma, diabetic retina, keratoconus or pediatric therapy — is structured so that you leave your first visit already knowing what happens next. We treat with empathy, and that begins with not leaving you in the dark.

  • Comprehensive services for all age groups, delivered with empathy
  • Corneal cross-linking for keratoconus — the only centre in Manipur
  • Myopia screening for school-going children and follow-up pathways
  • Glaucoma care emphasis for adults above 50 and at-risk patients
  • Retina screening support for diabetic patients

Community & education

Eye care that does not wait for people to come to us.

We run eye check-up workshops in schools across Manipur — taking our team and equipment onto the campus and screening every student, class by class. A child who cannot see the blackboard is too often mistaken for a child who is not paying attention, and a single afternoon of screening can change how that child learns for years.

We also take part in multi-specialty medical camps across the state, including a media-person health camp in July 2024 and a free mega medical camp in December 2025 that reached more than 200 beneficiaries.
