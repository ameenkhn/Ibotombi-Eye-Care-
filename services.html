Corneal cross-linking · C3R
The only such centre in all of Manipur

Keratoconus can be stopped — and the treatment is now here at home.

Keratoconus usually begins in the teenage years. The cornea — the clear front window of the eye — gradually thins and pushes outward into a cone shape. Vision becomes blurred and distorted, and each new pair of glasses helps a little less than the last.

Corneal collagen cross-linking is the one treatment proven to halt that progression. It does not undo damage already done, which is exactly why coming in early matters so much. Until now, patients in Manipur had to travel out of state for it. Today, Ibotombi Eye Care is the only hospital in Manipur that offers it.

Step 1

Corneal assessment

We confirm the diagnosis and check that your cornea is suitable for treatment.

Step 2

Riboflavin & UV light

Vitamin B2 drops are absorbed into the cornea, then activated by carefully measured UV light.

Step 3

Healing & review

You go home the same day, with scheduled reviews to confirm the cornea has stabilised.

  • Performed on the OMNI Cross Linking System with real-time eye tracking
  • Best suited to progressive keratoconus caught early
  • Day-care procedure — no travel outside Manipur
  • Every step explained to you beforehand, in your own language

Orthoptek Therapy

A newly added option for amblyopia and binocular vision support.

Orthoptek was installed at Ibotombi Eye Care on April 17, 2026. Based on the available published and clinical descriptions, it is a non-invasive treatment approach used in amblyopia and related binocular vision disorders through visual stimulation training.

The device has been described as aiming to stimulate parvocellular and magnocellular pathways and support improvement in monocular vision, stereopsis and binocular cooperation in selected patients. Suitability still depends on proper examination and clinical judgment.

  • Relevant for lazy eye / amblyopia discussions
  • Can be considered in selected binocular vision or stereopsis-related cases
  • Non-invasive therapy pathway
  • Integrated into the clinic's pediatric vision and therapy service mix

Orthoptics & vision therapy

Proper equipment for squint, lazy eye and binocular vision.

Training the two eyes to work together takes more than an eye chart. These are the instruments our orthoptics team uses to measure the problem accurately and then work on it, session by session.
